Localization and mapping provide the spatial foundation required for autonomous mobility. ASTRA studies multi-sensor positioning, localization integrity and map-based representations that allow autonomous systems to estimate their position reliably and interpret their surroundings. Research includes sensor fusion, fault detection and isolation, protection levels, georeferencing and the alignment of heterogeneous map and sensor information. The team also investigates the creation, maintenance and updating of high-definition maps using vehicle and crowdsourced observations. These established capabilities naturally extend toward richer spatial representations that connect localization, mapping and scene understanding.

## Representative topics

- Multi-sensor localization and asynchronous sensor fusion
- Localization integrity and uncertainty
- Fault detection, isolation and protection levels
- Georeferencing with accessible sensing systems
- Alignment of camera, LiDAR and map information
- High-definition map creation and updating
- Crowdsourced mapping

## Emerging directions

- Semantic and vectorized environment representations
- Online and incremental mapping
- Dynamic spatial representations
- Learning-based map construction and updating
