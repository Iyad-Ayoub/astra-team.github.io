Cross-cutting methods support several ASTRA research axes rather than forming a separate fifth axis. The team explores these approaches when they can strengthen perception, localization and mapping, decision-making, control, simulation and mobility-system analysis. Established methodological foundations include robustness, uncertainty handling, multimodal learning, physical modelling, optimization and simulation. More recent AI paradigms are treated as emerging directions whose relevance is assessed through concrete autonomous-mobility problems rather than as standalone research programmes.

## Strongly anchored cross-cutting themes

- Robustness, uncertainty and reliability
- Multimodal learning and representation
- Physics-informed and data-driven methods
- Simulation and traffic-system modelling
- Generative approaches where relevant

## Emerging strategic directions

- Foundation Models and Multimodal AI
- Vision-Language Models
- Vision-Language-Action Models
- World Models and Dynamic World Representations
- Synthetic Data and Scenario Generation
- Agentic and Multi-Agent Intelligence
