Decision-making and vehicle control form a continuous chain from understanding the traffic situation to executing safe and feasible vehicle motion. ASTRA studies how autonomous vehicles can anticipate the behavior of surrounding agents, reason under uncertainty, select appropriate maneuvers, plan trajectories and control vehicle dynamics. Research combines behavior and trajectory prediction with optimization, reinforcement learning, inverse reinforcement learning and physics-based models. Particular attention is given to safety, comfort, energy efficiency, traffic rules and robustness to uncertain or partially observed situations. This integrated perspective connects prediction and reasoning directly to planning and control.

## Representative topics

- Situation understanding under uncertainty
- Behavior, maneuver and trajectory prediction
- Decision-making under uncertainty
- Motion and trajectory planning
- Reinforcement and inverse reinforcement learning
- Vehicle dynamics and robust control
- Safety, comfort, energy efficiency and rule compliance

## Emerging directions

- Joint prediction and planning
- Learning-based reasoning
- Uncertainty propagation across prediction, planning and control
- World-model-based decision and planning
