ASTRA participated in the 29th IEEE International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC 2026), held in Naples, Italy, from September 15 to 18, 2026.

Islem Kobbi presented the paper “Motion Planning in Urban Environments via Self-Play Reinforcement Learning,” co-authored with Tiago Rocha Gonçalves and Fawzi Nashashibi, ASTRA team leader.

The research investigates reinforcement-learning-based motion planning for autonomous vehicles in complex urban environments. In particular, it explores a self-play learning strategy, in which driving agents improve their planning behaviour through repeated interactions, with the objective of developing more adaptive decision-making for challenging traffic situations.

The work contributes to ASTRA’s research on prediction, decision-making, planning and control for autonomous driving, and was presented as part of the scientific programme of IEEE ITSC 2026.

More information about the conference is available on the [IEEE ITSC 2026 website](https://ieee-itsc.org/2026/).
