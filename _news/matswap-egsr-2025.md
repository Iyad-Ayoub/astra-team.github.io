MatSwap received a Best Paper Honorable Mention at EGSR 2025. The work explores light-aware material transfer in images and was developed through collaboration between ASTRA and Adobe researchers.

The work was developed by Ivan Lopes, Valentin Deschaintre, Yannick Hold-Geoffroy and Raoul de Charette.
