ASTRA is a joint research team between [Inria](https://www.inria.fr/) and [Valeo](https://www.valeo.com/), dedicated to intelligent transportation systems and automated driving. The team conducts research across perception, localization and mapping, prediction, decision-making, planning, vehicle control, and large-scale mobility systems.

Its objective is to contribute to safer, more efficient, and more sustainable mobility by combining fundamental research with experimental validation and real-world transportation challenges. ASTRA studies autonomous navigation, connected and cooperative mobility, multimodal sensing, and traffic-system intelligence, while developing methods that connect vehicle-level autonomy with the broader mobility ecosystem.

The team brings together complementary expertise in physical and mathematical modelling, optimization, machine learning, and data-driven methods to address the complexity of intelligent and autonomous transportation.
