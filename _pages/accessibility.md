ASTRA aims to make this website accessible and is progressively reviewing its accessibility.

No ASTRA-specific formal accessibility audit is available to support a compliance status or score. This notice does not claim RGAA compliance or partial compliance.

## Report a problem

If you have difficulty accessing content or using a feature, contact the current team assistant, [Christelle Leclerc](mailto:krystel.leclerc@inria.fr). Please identify the page, describe the difficulty and, if useful, mention your browser or assistive technology so the team can investigate and help you access the information.

See [Inria's broader accessibility commitment](https://www.inria.fr/fr/declaration-accessibilite). Its declaration concerns the Inria website and is not an audit of this ASTRA site.
