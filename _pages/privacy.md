## Public content and contact

This static website has no user accounts or contact form. It does not directly collect personal data through forms or account registration.

The site publishes professional information about team members and research activities, including names, roles, affiliations, portraits, publication authors and news references. For corrections to information about you, contact [Christelle Leclerc](mailto:krystel.leclerc@inria.fr).

Email links open your email application. If you contact the team, your address and message are used to handle your enquiry; the message is not submitted through this website.

## Hosting and external services

GitHub Pages hosts the site. Hosting requests can expose technical information such as IP addresses and browser details to the provider; ASTRA does not directly control GitHub's logs. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Pages load fonts and interface resources from Google Fonts and content-delivery networks including jsDelivr and unpkg. The contact map loads OpenStreetMap tiles. These requests transmit technical information to those providers when the resources load, not only when you follow a link. Other external links lead to services with their own privacy policies.

## Cookies and trackers

No audience-measurement or advertising trackers are currently enabled by ASTRA on this website. Google Analytics is disabled. No analytics consent banner is currently presented; this is not a claim that every third-party service is cookie-free.

## Personal-data enquiries

For questions about personal data or exercising applicable data-protection rights, contact Inria's Data Protection Officer at [dpo@inria.fr](mailto:dpo@inria.fr). See [Inria's data-protection information](https://www.inria.fr/fr/protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-rgpd) for its institutional framework and [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/) for information about your rights.
